STEMEd+ Awards

Nomination calendar Self-nominations welcome New listings (RSS) Suggest an award

STEMEd+ Awards is part of STEMEd+ Commons, which brings together teachers, researchers, and learning professionals from every field to share work, find each other, and build what comes next. Join to set up your profile, add papers, posters, slides, and other work to the STEMEd+ Library (opens in a new tab) with a DOI, share datasets in STEMEd+ Data (opens in a new tab), and connect with teachers and researchers across fields. You can sign in through your institution, ORCID, or another provider.

Nominations closing in the next 60 days

Deadlines, eligibility, and rules change. Always confirm details on the sponsor's own page before you nominate.

    Nomination calendar

    Subscribe to every dated nomination deadline in Google Calendar, Outlook, or Apple Calendar with this address (choose "add calendar from URL"):

    Each listing with a deadline also has an "Add to calendar" link for just that deadline. Most awards need letters of support, so plan a few weeks ahead.

    What is listed

    national and international awards for STEM+ teaching, STEM+ education research, and education-adjacent work such as science communication and public engagement, kept by hand and each checked against the sponsor's page on the date shown. Awards between cycles stay listed so you can plan a nomination. Campus and regional awards are not listed, except where they lead to a national one.

    Disciplines use the same categories as every STEMEd+ board. Awards open to all STEM+ fields appear under every discipline.

    Add or correct a listing

    Know an award we are missing, or see something out of date? Suggest an award or report a correction on GitHub, or use our contact form and include the award's name, the sponsor's page, and the deadline. For a correction, name the award you are correcting.

        About this site ·

        This site is part of STEMEd+ Commons, a Commons within Knowledge Commons. STEMEd+ Commons brings together teachers, researchers, and learning professionals from every field to share work, find each other, and build what comes next. As an open access platform, STEMEd+ Commons helps ensure STEM+ teaching and STEM+ education research are accessible for all people, in all communities. This material is based upon work supported by the National Science Foundation under grants OAC-2226271, DRL-2321946, OAC-2531819 and by Michigan State University through the Jenison Fund.
